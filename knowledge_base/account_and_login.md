# ClothesSpot – Account Management & Login Help

## 1. Password Reset Instructions
If you forgot your password or cannot log into your ClothesSpot account, follow these simple steps to regain access:
1. Navigate to the **ClothesSpot Login** page.
2. Click on the **"Forgot Password?"** link below the login form.
3. Enter the email address associated with your ClothesSpot account and click **"Send Reset Link"**.
4. Check your email inbox for an email from `support@clothesspot.com` with the subject *"Reset Your ClothesSpot Password"*. (If you do not see it within 2 minutes, please check your Spam or Junk folder).
5. Click the secure reset link inside the email. Note: For security reasons, the link expires in **15 minutes**.
6. Enter and confirm your new password, then click **"Save and Log In"**.

## 2. Password Security Requirements
Your new password must satisfy the following criteria:
- Minimum of 8 characters in length.
- At least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
- At least one lowercase letter (a-z).
- At least one numeric digit (0-9).
- At least one special symbol (e.g., !@#$%^&*).

## 3. Account Lockout Protection
- For your protection, accounts are temporarily locked for **30 minutes** after 5 consecutive incorrect password attempts.
- You can either wait 30 minutes for automatic unlock or use the "Forgot Password" link immediately to reset your credentials.

## 4. Managing Account Details
- Once logged in, you can update your shipping addresses, phone number, and communication preferences under **Account Settings > Profile**.
