# ClothesSpot – Returns & Refund Policy

## 1. 30-Day Return Window
At ClothesSpot, customer satisfaction is our top priority. You may return any eligible item within **30 days of the delivery date** for a full refund to your original payment method or store credit.

## 2. Item Condition & Eligibility
- Items must be in their original, unworn, unwashed, and undamaged condition.
- All original tags must remain attached.
- Footwear and shoes must include the original shoe box undamaged and placed inside an outer protective shipping box.
- Final sale clearance items, intimate apparel/underwear, and customized products are non-returnable.

## 3. How to Return Items and Get a Refund (Step-by-Step)
To initiate a return and receive your refund:
1. Log into your account at **ClothesSpot.com** and navigate to **My Orders**.
2. Locate the order containing the item (e.g., shoes or clothing) you wish to return and click **Request Return / Refund**.
3. Select the reason for return and choose your preferred refund method (Original Payment Method or Store Credit + 10% bonus).
4. Download and print the **prepaid return shipping label** generated for your return.
5. Securely pack the item in its original box, attach the prepaid label, and drop it off at any authorized courier location or schedule a pickup.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeline
- Once our fulfillment warehouse receives your return package, our quality assurance team inspects the item within **2 to 3 business days**.
- Following inspection approval, your refund is immediately processed back to your original payment method.
- Bank processing typically takes **5 to 7 business days** for the credited funds to appear on your bank or credit card statement.
