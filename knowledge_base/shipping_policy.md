# ClothesSpot – Shipping & Delivery Policy

## 1. Shipping Methods & Delivery Timelines
ClothesSpot offers reliable shipping across all domestic and international destinations:
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days ($4.99 flat rate, or FREE on orders over $50.00).
- **Express Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days ($12.99 flat rate).
- **Next-Day Delivery**: Guaranteed delivery on the next business day for orders placed before 12:00 PM EST ($19.99 flat rate).
- **International Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance.

## 2. Order Tracking
- Once your order is dispatched from our fulfillment center, you will receive an automated dispatch confirmation email containing your courier name and a live tracking link.
- You can also track your package anytime by logging into your ClothesSpot account, navigating to **My Orders**, and selecting **Track Package**.

## 3. What to Do If Your Order Is Delayed
- If your package has not arrived by the estimated delivery date (for example, if it was supposed to arrive yesterday):
  1. Please check the tracking link in your dispatch email for courier transit scans or weather delays.
  2. Couriers occasionally mark a package as out for delivery and complete delivery within the next 24 to 48 business hours.
  3. If tracking has not updated for more than 48 hours past the estimated delivery date, please reply to your order email or contact support with your order number. Our team will initiate an expedited trace with the courier and issue a free replacement or full refund if the package is confirmed delayed or lost.

## 4. Address Changes & Order Modifications
- Delivery addresses can be updated within 1 hour of order placement directly from your account page. Once an order enters the packing stage, address changes cannot be made.
