# ClothesSpot – Product FAQ, Replacements & Promotional Rules

## 1. Broken, Damaged, or Defective Items Replacement Process
If you receive an item that arrived broken, defective, or damaged (such as an apparel accessory, lifestyle device, or kitchen blender):
- You are entitled to a **free immediate replacement** if reported within **14 days of delivery**.
- **How to request a replacement**:
  1. Go to **My Orders** on ClothesSpot.
  2. Select the order containing the broken item and click **"Report Damaged Item / Request Replacement"**.
  3. Upload 1 or 2 clear photos showing the damage or defect and the outer packaging.
  4. Select **"Request Replacement Item"**.
  5. Our system automatically dispatches a brand-new replacement within **24 to 48 hours** at no additional charge, along with a prepaid return shipping label to return the damaged item.

## 2. Gift Cards and Corporate Bulk Purchasing Policy
- ClothesSpot sells standard digital gift cards available on our website in set denominations of $10, $25, $50, and $100 for individual consumers.
- **Corporate Bulk / Custom Gift Cards**: ClothesSpot **does not** offer custom corporate branding, company-branded gift cards, or bulk B2B gift card programs for organizations (e.g., 500 employee orders with company logos). If an inquiry requests corporate branding or bulk enterprise vouchers, support agents must honestly state that we do not offer this service and escalate the request to our B2B inquiries team.

## 3. Discount Vouchers & Promo Codes
- Only one promotional code or voucher can be applied per checkout order.
- Promo codes (e.g., SUMMER25) are valid on regular-priced items with a minimum order subtotal of $50, and cannot be stacked with clearance or flash sales.

## 4. Product Quality Guarantee
All non-clothing electronic and lifestyle accessories sold on ClothesSpot are backed by a 1-year limited warranty against internal component defects.
