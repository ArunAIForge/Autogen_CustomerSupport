# ClothesSpot – Billing, Payments & Dispute Policy

## 1. Accepted Payment Methods
ClothesSpot accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Klarna pay-in-4 installments.

## 2. Duplicate or Unexpected Card Charges
- If your statement shows multiple charges for the same order, this is often a temporary pre-authorization hold placed by your card issuer. Pre-authorizations typically drop off within 24 to 48 business hours.
- If multiple settled charges have been posted to your bank or card account:
  1. Please contact our Billing Support Team immediately with your order ID or transaction references.
  2. Our finance department will review the merchant ledger and issue an immediate manual refund for any duplicate settled transaction within 1 business day.
- For unresolved payment disputes or potential unauthorized transactions, our specialized fraud and escalations team handles cases with priority resolution.

## 3. Fraud and Unauthorized Access Protection
- If you suspect someone has accessed your account or placed orders without your consent, ClothesSpot's Trust & Safety team will instantly freeze the account, cancel pending unauthorized shipments, and reverse any fraudulent charges.
